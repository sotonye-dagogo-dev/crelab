How It Works

Everything you need to know to get started — whether you're a creator looking for work or a brand searching for talent.

For Creators

Turn your creativity into income
1

Create Your Profile

Showcase your work, set your rates, and define your niche. Add portfolio items from your device or sync from Google Drive.

2

Get Discovered

Brands browse our Explore feed and search for creators like you. Your profile appears in relevant searches based on your category, location, and tags.

3

Receive Booking Requests

Clients send booking requests with project details. You can accept, decline, or negotiate scope and timeline.

4

Deliver & Get Paid

Work is protected by escrow. Funds are held securely and released automatically after completion (or when you mark milestones as done).

For Clients & Brands

Find the right creative talent, fast
1

Browse & Search

Explore creators by category, location, experience level, and budget. Watch video portfolios to evaluate style and quality.

2

Book with Confidence

Select a package, set a date, and pay securely. Your payment is held in escrow until the work is delivered to your satisfaction.

3

Collaborate Seamlessly

Communicate directly with your creator, share briefs, and track progress. Milestone payments keep larger projects on track.

4

Review & Release

Review deliverables, request revisions if needed, and release payment. Leave a review to help the community.

Secure Payments with Escrow

Your money is protected every step of the way
1

Payment Held in Escrow

When you book, funds are securely held by our payment partner (Paystack) — not released to the creator until you're happy.

2

Auto-Release Timeline

If no dispute is raised, funds auto-release 5 days after the service date. You're always in control.

3

Dispute Resolution

If something goes wrong, our admin team mediates. We review evidence from both sides and make a fair decision.

4

Milestone Payments

For larger projects, split payments into milestones. Each milestone is funded upfront and released upon approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions — improving SEO and reducing support load

Payments
How do I get paid as a creator?
Payments are held in escrow via Paystack. Once the client approves your work (or after 5 days with no dispute), funds are automatically released to your CreLab wallet. You can withdraw to your bank account anytime.
Payments
What's the platform fee?
CreLab charges a 5% platform fee on each booking. This covers payment processing, escrow protection, platform maintenance, and support.
Bookings
Can I cancel a booking?
Yes. Full refunds are available within 48 hours of booking. After that, a 50% cancellation fee applies to protect the creator's time.
Creators
How do I build my portfolio?
Upload videos and images directly, or connect Google Drive to sync your existing portfolio. Portfolio items appear on your public profile and in the Explore feed.
Clients
What types of creators can I hire?
We support Content Creators (UGC, lifestyle, brand content) and Cinematographers/Videographers (events, commercials, narrative, documentary). Each has tailored profile fields.
Security
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use bank-grade encryption, row-level database security, and comply with NDPR 2023. Payment data is handled by Paystack (PCI DSS Level 1 certified).
